First Principle
Plūrimīs Prōdesse
/ˈpluː.ri.miːs proːˈdes.se/
"To benefit the most people."

From the classical Latin prōdesse — to be of use, to do good, to serve — paired with plūrimīs, the dative plural of "the most." A phrase rooted in Cicero's writings on civic duty and the common good.

This is the epicenter of Belivor. Every venture, every decision, every line of code radiates outward from a single test: does this help the most people?

It's not a tagline. It's the gravity that holds the system together. Companies are vehicles. People are the point.

— Belivor, founding principle
